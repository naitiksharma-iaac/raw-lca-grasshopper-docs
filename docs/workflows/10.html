Comparing alternatives

Equivalent quantity + alternative materials → separate Results

Author and interpret

Use equivalent functional units, method and boundaries. Three 300 kg Tabbycrete panels with lime fractions .20/.25/.30 yield historical modeled CO₂ values 41.58/51.975/62.37 under the original assumptions, not validated complete GWP. Use standard Grasshopper comparisons; do not sum alternative designs as one physical assembly.

Coverage check

Inspect Result for units, supplier coverage, factor context and missing contributions. Available numerical impacts are not proof of scientific completeness.
