Ladybug & Honeybee component study

Twelve exact component/icon pairs from official repositories at pinned revisions. Reference icons remain hosted upstream; no third-party source archive or implementation is copied into this repository. The observations and RAW interpretations are distinct.

LB Import EPW

Function: Reads an EPW weather file into a Location and weather data collections.

Observed graphic: Red file-like rectangle with large black EPW lettering and a small upper-corner mark.

RAW interpretation: The familiar file-format name is more specific than a general weather symbol. RAW can use GWP or source initials when the text is the object of interest.

LB Construct Location

Function: Creates a Location from name, latitude, longitude, time zone and elevation.

Observed graphic: Black I / NY lettering with a red heart, referencing the familiar I-love-NY composition.

RAW interpretation: Location is suggested by a place-name reference. The icon alone does not disclose the input schema; tooltip and paired deconstructor complete its meaning.

LB Deconstruct Location

Function: Extracts name, latitude, longitude, time zone and elevation from a Location.

Observed graphic: Same I / NY arrangement, with a red X replacing the heart.

RAW interpretation: A small shared-motif change distinguishes opposing operations. X can also imply error/deletion: RAW should make decomposition direction clearer rather than copy it.

LB SunPath

Function: Produces sun-path geometry and sun vectors for location and times.

Observed graphic: Yellow circular disc, cross-like divisions and a red curved trace.

RAW interpretation: The icon resembles the kind of solar diagram produced. It need not reproduce every analemma to cue the output.

LB Create Legend

Function: Creates colour swatches, label objects/text and colours for values/range.

Observed graphic: Vertical red-to-blue swatches with black marks/numerals alongside.

RAW interpretation: A compact image of the output is directly meaningful. Colour here encodes a scale, not a claim that every low value is good.

LB Area Normalize

Function: Divides a compatible data collection by an area and changes the data type/unit; invalid types or zero area fail.

Observed graphic: Two-tone red/pink rectangle with a horizontal divide and white m² below.

RAW interpretation: The denominator names the operation. RAW Physical Properties should not imply normalisation simply because it exposes area.

HB Room

Function: Combines Honeybee Faces into a Room with optional construction/modifier/program settings.

Observed graphic: Yellow/orange isometric enclosed room with cyan window faces and dark outlines.

RAW interpretation: Recognisable room is the shared object family. The source function is construction even without an assembly arrow in the icon.

HB Room from Solid

Function: Converts closed Rhino solids/meshes into Honeybee Rooms, with optional model properties.

Observed graphic: Same room silhouette, but its top is grey instead of yellow.

RAW interpretation: The subtle top change distinguishes a related constructor. It is legible as a family, but exact grey-top semantics require labels; RAW should not over-rely on tiny changes.

HB Deconstruct Object

Function: Returns constituent Faces, Apertures, Doors and Shades from Honeybee geometry objects.

Observed graphic: Room faces are separated around pink/red junction lines and larger gaps.

RAW interpretation: Separated pieces visually reinforce extracting child objects. RAW must extract properties, not copy an exploded room or suggest it physically disassembles material.

HB Solve Adjacency

Function: Finds matching faces between Rooms and assigns boundary relationships, with explicit options.

Observed graphic: Two yellow/orange cells, a central grey division and black inward-facing marks.

RAW interpretation: The interface between objects carries the meaning. It is not merely a building picture; RAW assignment needs equally explicit joining/ledger logic without a verification tick.

HB Model

Function: Collects Rooms and permitted orphaned geometry into a simulation Model.

Observed graphic: Multi-storey building-like stack with cyan bands, yellow facade and grey top.

RAW interpretation: More complex aggregate distinguishes Model from a single Room. RAW should reserve assembly complexity for an actual composite rather than decorate every icon.

HB Geometry Properties

Function: Reads wall/window area, volume and floor-area measures from Rooms or Model.

Observed graphic: Room silhouette with dimension-like lines extending along its right and lower sides.

RAW interpretation: Dimension marks distinguish reading quantities from constructing the room. RAW can use a block with distinct attribute outputs instead of an editor metaphor.
