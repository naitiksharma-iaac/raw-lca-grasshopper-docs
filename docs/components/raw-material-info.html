Materials

RAW Material Info

Inspects original, custom or derived material metadata.

Inputs: Material

Outputs: Name; Source; Declared Quantity; Region; Lifecycle Modules; A1-A3 GWP; Density; Evidence / Type; Method Summary; Indicator Summary; Context Summary

Read-only; not the element physical inspector.

Use and limitations

Optional and mode-specific ports may remain serialized when hidden. Inspect quantities, source provenance and coverage before interpreting results. A missing input is not an inferred zero.
