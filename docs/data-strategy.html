Data strategy

Trace every value to its source.

Environmental records need provenance, units, scope, methods, versions, and permissions.

No environmental dataset is bundled. ecoinvent-derived data is not included.

V1

  • Local-first and no-server.
  • User-created or user-imported records.
  • Manual EPD entry and local CSV/JSON exchange may be explored.
  • Examples only from synthetic, openly licensed, or explicitly permitted sources.

Future interoperability

LCAx, ILCD+EPD, openEPD, and API connectors may be explored. Each adapter would need documented mappings, version support, validation, and source-compliant caching. User credentials must remain local and private.

Hosted services

A hosted database, sync service, or team library may be explored only if funding and licensing allow. Local operation must remain possible.
