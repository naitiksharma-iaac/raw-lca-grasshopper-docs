Architecture

Small, local, and traceable.

The proposed design separates geometry integration, reviewed calculation logic, and data access.

No finished plugin is released yet. These are proposed boundaries, not implemented features.

Proposed layers

  • Grasshopper interface: inputs, outputs, warnings, and geometry-linked feedback.
  • Domain layer: quantities, units, scenarios, provenance, and validated calculations.
  • Data adapters: local records first; optional format and service connectors later.

V1 boundary

V1 is local-first and no-server. It would use user-managed records and local import/export. It would bundle no environmental dataset, including ecoinvent-derived data.

Possible extensions

Future LCAx, ILCD+EPD, openEPD, and API connectors may be explored without coupling the core workflow to one provider. A future hosted platform is conditional on funding and licensing and must remain optional.
